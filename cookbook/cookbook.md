# Brock's Cookbook

### Asian-Inspired Ground Beef & Rice Bowl

Cook 1lb ground beef and 1 cup (uncooked) of jasime rice. Top with sriracha, sliced cucumbers, and sesame seeds.

### Stone Ground Mustard Ground Beef & Cinnamon Sweet Potatoes

Cook 1lb ground beef. Season sweet potatoes with salt and cinnamon, topped with a 1tbsp of oil. Airfry potatoes @ 400F for 12 minutes. Top ground beef with stone ground mustard (add honey if desired). Char grape/cherry tomatoes in pan with beef if desired.
