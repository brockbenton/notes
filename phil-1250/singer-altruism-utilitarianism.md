# Peter Singer

## The why and how of effective altruism

Singer presents a though experiment: Say a little girl is ran over by a van in a street. If you are walking by, would you help? Most people would say yes - but Singer asks the following question: Does it really matter that you don't have to walk around these children as you walk down your street? Millions of children die per year, from known-to-be preventable diseases. Can we reduce that death toll?

Much of us spend money on things we don't *need*. Instead, we could give up our money to various charities that are aimed at reducing the exact death toll discussed in the thought experiment.

*My Opposition: Everything is self-serving in the technical sense. But even with that, something can be self-serving, and also societally-serving.*
