# Patriotism

### Robert E. Goodin on Patriotism

Patriotism is a special affection for one's own country; a sense of personal identification with the country; a special concern for the well-being of the country; and a willingness to sacrifice to promote the country's good.

What makes our fellow countrymen special is not an appeal to national borders (the fact that we are in the same country), but instead that we are subject to a list of *special duties*. These special duties are owed by certain people to other people due to a relationship (family, friends, citizenship to the same country). 

### Howard Zinn on Patriotism

The American people have fallen in a trap in which they believe that patriotism is believing in whatever the government does - and any sort of criticism of the government is unpatriotic. This would undermine basic democratic principles, particularly those that we founded our nation on. Governments are artificial creations by individuals that are aimed at the protection of rights. When the government fails to do it's duty, then it is up to the people to abolish and/or reform the government. This duty by the people naturally implies the necessity for criticism - and is thus, not unpatriotic.

### Robert Reich on Patriotism

Nationalism is about exclusion (supremacy and fear of outsiders); patriotism is about inclusion (taking on a fair share of the burden that requires the country to continue on). 

### Dan Rather on Patriotism

Nationalism is a monologue on which you place your country in a position of moral and cultural supremacy; patriotism is a dialogue with your fellow citizens and the larger world.
