# Thomas Hobbes, *Leviathan* 

Thesis: To what extent should we patiently obey rulers, and to what extension should we start revolutions? This thesis came from a heavy American-Revolution-perspective. 

*Leviathan:* People should obey government - even those led by imperfect rulers - to avoid chaos and bloodshed. 

The *Leviathan* was a reaction to Hobbes' disagreement with the *Divine Rule* philosophy - primarily due to the decline of religion in Western societies. (Hobbes was privately an Atheist). Additionally, Hobbes was worried that the *Social Contract Theory* would allow too much government opposition, occurring anytime society didn't like something that the government did. 

*The State of Nature:* Before there were kings of anytime, "society" was not a pretty place that commonly descended into fighting and bickering. 

The only time an individual could rightfully oppose a ruler would be if the ruler threatened to kill them.
