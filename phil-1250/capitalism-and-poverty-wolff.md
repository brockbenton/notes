# Is Capitalism Actually Reducing Poverty? (with Richard Wolff)

According to the world bank, anyone making under $1.90 a day is considered "poor." But that number isn't actually based on the living costs of any actual person. If we use the number suggested by the United Nations which is $7.90, then poverty has risen to 4.2 billion and has increased distinctly. The only country where we have seen noticeable improvement in poverty rates is China. This was accomplished with massive government industrial spending and social programs.  

"No advanced economy has ever achieved low poverty rates without high levels of government social spending."
