# James Baldwin on Education & Youth (1963)

Notes are based on: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69pi63RgeJ4&t=431s

---

* When a child is born, their identity is solely their mothers; and the moment they realize that they (the child) and their mother share a commonality of individual existence, it is the first moment of education.
* A society is under the obligation to education the youth.
  * Take the example of the children of the Third Reich.
* A teacher (the old) depends on the student (the young) as much as the student depends on the teacher.
  * A student ought to ask: "In what ways are my education relevant to the concrete problems of our society?" (e.g. simple tasks such as buying an apartment, as well as larger issues like social injustices) 
* When a person discusses "culture," they are not *actually* discussing culture, but rather **power**. 
  * The difference between the African cultures (a set of vanishing cultures) and the European cultures (the dominant set of cultures) is that the Europeans had the power.
* A black nationalist is creating the same destructive path that the white nationalists created.
  * Words mean something: What is a black nationalist?
